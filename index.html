Yejin Yu 유예진

Ph.D. Student in Information Systems
Yonsei University School of Business

I study how digital platforms and streaming content shape offline economic activity, such as local consumption, commercial districts, and international travel. My work combines causal inference designs (difference-in-differences, event studies, and instrumental variables) with large-scale card transaction data.

Before my Ph.D., I spent about six years as a data analyst at SK Telecom, working on AI strategy, advertising, and metaverse platforms. I hold an M.S. in Applied Data Science from the University of Michigan and a B.A. in Theology and B.S. in Computer Science from Yonsei University.

I am advised by Professor Daegon Cho.
